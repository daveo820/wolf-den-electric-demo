Services

Eight things Matt gets called for.

Taken from Wolf Den’s own service list, plus the porch and sunroom work that fills the permit record.

01

Repairs and troubleshooting

Dead outlets, a doorbell that stopped ringing, a dishwasher with no power. One reviewer’s doorbell and dishwasher both work again.

02

Porch and sunroom electrical

Outlets, lighting and floodlights for screen porches closed in as three season rooms, glass rooms and new deck roofs. All on the permit record.

03

Lighting and LED upgrades

New fixtures, recessed and LED lighting, indoor and outdoor.

04

Wiring upgrades

Bring older wiring up to current code, and fix what the last electrician left behind.

Backup generators

Generator hookups for homes and businesses so the power stays on in an outage.

Ethernet for the home

Wired network runs for one room or the whole house.

07

Insurance inspections

A licensed look at the electrical system when the insurer asks for one.

08

Commercial and industrial

Energy efficient upgrades, maintenance and equipment installs for businesses and facilities.
