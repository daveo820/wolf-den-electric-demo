# Wolf Den Electric: concept site by LuminArch

A pitch demo for Wolf Den Electric, LLC (Durham, NC). **Not the official site.** Noindexed, blocked in robots.txt, and labeled with a concept banner on every page.

Wolf Den's old domain, wolfdenelectric.com, now belongs to someone else and redirects visitors to affiliate and spam pages. This concept is built to launch on a new domain. `wolfdenelectricnc.com` is used throughout as a labeled placeholder; nothing has been registered.

- Live preview: https://daveo820.github.io/wolf-den-electric-demo/
- Pages: home, services, reviews, about, get a quote, 404
- Stack: static HTML, CSS and one small JS file, generated by `build.py` (run `python3 build.py`).
- Facts and sources: `FACTS_LOG.md`. Design: `DESIGN_DNA.md`. Research and the domain timeline: `market-research.md`.
- Screenshots: `screenshots/index-desktop-fold.png` and `screenshots/index-mobile-fold.png`.

Before launch: Matt registers a new domain, the Google Business Profile website link gets changed to it, the concept banner and noindex come off, the form gets connected, and every TODO-VERIFY in the facts log is cleared.
