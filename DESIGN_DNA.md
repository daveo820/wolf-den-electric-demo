# Design DNA: Wolf Den Electric concept

- **Archetype:** magazine cover. A giant outlined "WOLF DEN" masthead behind everything, the headline and cover copy on the left, and Matt's own photo as the cover image on the right, tilted, in a white border with a red offset shadow. Three cover lines (5.0, 30, Vet) sit across the photo edge. Not used in any earlier LuminArch demo.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (width 75 to 88%, weight 800) for display and labels. Literata for body at 18px, a reading serif, which is rare in this trade. Neither was used before.
- **Color story (sampled from the Wolf Den logo):** den `#19191b` (cover, dark bands), charcoal `#545555` (logo gray), pelt `#8d877f`, bone `#eee9e1` (paper panels), snow `#faf8f5` (page), wolf `#d61f42` (logo red: buttons, marks), wolf dk `#a8172f` (small red text on light), plus a placeholder yellow tag `#ffd23f` used only to mark placeholders.
- **Signature element:** the Triangle mark, a small solid red triangle for the Research Triangle. It is the kicker mark, the review sign off, the list bullet, the "Heads up" mark and the footer fact bullet.
- **Motion personality:** ink settle. Blocks fade in, rise 24px and clear a 3px blur over 0.7s, like ink drying. Hover on buttons drops a hard offset shadow. Off under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Grid breaks:** home, the red "12 of 12" column bleeds past the left edge of the wrap; about, Matt's photo hangs 140px out of the dark page head into the body.
- **Footer:** "Back page". CALL MATT. in condensed caps, the number in red at display size, four facts, and a plain note about the old web address.
- **Distinct from Triple Crown:** dark cover vs light paper, serif body vs sans, red and charcoal vs navy and gold, photo led vs diagram led.
